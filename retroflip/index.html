A flip clock for the bedside

RetroFlip

Android · a personal project

An app that turns an Android phone on a bedside table into a flip-clock dock: the time from across the room, the weather and the day's events, a spoken good morning, sleep sounds, and an optional security camera that only watches while its owner's iPhone is away from home.

It's written for my own phone and isn't in any app store. With the camera on, it can keep clips in the owner's own Google Drive and send alerts from their own Gmail; the privacy policy says exactly how.
